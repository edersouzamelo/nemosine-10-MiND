MiND source and binary distributions may include third-party components. Each
component remains governed by its own license and attribution requirements.

The Windows release workflow generates the release-specific dependency and
license inventory from the environment used to build the installer. That
generated inventory is packaged with the application as
THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.txt and must be reviewed before a public release.
